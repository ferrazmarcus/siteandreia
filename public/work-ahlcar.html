Ahlçar is a e-commerce of exclusive handmade genuine leather bags in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.
The brand's symbol merges a bag strap icon with the letter "A," using minimalist lines and consistent typography.
The chosen typography reflects modernity, self-expression, and minimalism, suitable for small sizes prints and mobile use. The visual identity uses colors representing humanity, warmth, and Brazilian culture, portraying an authentic Brazilian fashion brand.
